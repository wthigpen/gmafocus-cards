6 · Print at home

Print several players' cards on one sheet

Add approved card files (from the GMA Focus Cards folder in Google Drive). They print together, one of each, filling as many sheets as needed.

    Settings for a Canon PRO-1100
    • In the print dialog pick the same paper size (Letter), set Scale 100% / Actual size (not "Fit"), and turn off headers and footers.
    • Load 320 gsm cardstock and holo sheets through the rear manual feed, one sheet at a time, and set the media type in the Canon driver to the closest heavy matte or photo type your paper maker recommends.
    • Holo paper: make sure it is rated for pigment inkjet (the PRO-1100 uses pigment ink), and let sheets dry before cutting. Ink on silver foil looks metallic; that is expected.
    • First time: print on plain paper and measure one card. It should be exactly 2.5″×3.5″.
    • Cut on the crop marks with a guillotine or rotary trimmer, then round the corners with a 1/8″ (3 mm) corner punch.
